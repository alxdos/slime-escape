# Content Archetypes from Markdown

- Status: done
- Created: 2026-04-23
- Updated: 2026-04-23 (done; T12 demo mutation smoke through MD → `content:build` → generated output; the dev server returned HTTP 200; demo edits reverted)

## Game designer

- Sees: three new MD files in `content/` — `content/weapons.md`, `content/drops.md`, `content/bosses.md` — each in the same format as `content/enemies.md` from story 011 (definitions partition + `# Balance` partition with narrow comparison tables). Additionally: in `content/enemies.md` the `## Sounds` and `## Voice` sections are filled in so that `hit`, `death`, and `voice` reference **different** slime sample pools.
- Can do: open the relevant MD, edit a value (for example `pistol.cooldownMs`, `heal-orb.effect.amount`, `slime-king.maxHp`, or `slime-fast.voice.sampleIds`), run `npm run content:build` and `npm run dev`, immediately see/hear the change in the game. Typos and references to non-existent `id`s are caught by the generator with clear messages and do not corrupt the working config.

## Technical

- The generation pipeline, MD format, and `<area>.ts ↔ <area>.generated.ts` rules stay the same — pinned in [../design/content-authoring.md](../design/content-authoring.md). This story reuses the existing `scripts/content-build/` generator and adds three new areas next to the working `enemies` area.
- Layer mapping:
  - **weapons** → `src/shared/content/weapons.generated.ts` (consumer — `weapons.ts` with the `WeaponArchetype` type and the `WEAPON_ARCHETYPES` registry) and `src/main/audio/weaponAudio.generated.ts` (consumer — `AudioMappings.ts`, the type owner of `WEAPON_AUDIO_MAPPINGS`);
  - **drops** → `src/shared/content/drops.generated.ts` (consumer — `drops.ts` with the `DropArchetype`/`DropEffect` types and the `DROP_ARCHETYPES` registry); there is no audio counterpart — `EVENT_AUDIO_MAPPINGS.dropPickup` stays inline in `AudioMappings.ts` and is not part of this story;
  - **bosses** → `src/shared/content/bosses.generated.ts` (consumer — `bosses.ts` with the `BossArchetype` type and the `BOSS_ARCHETYPES` registry) and `src/main/audio/bossAudio.generated.ts` (consumer — `AudioMappings.ts`, the type owner of `BOSS_AUDIO_MAPPINGS`).
- The shapes of `WeaponArchetype`, `DropArchetype`, `DropEffect`, `BossArchetype`, `BossPhaseSpec`, `BossAttackSpec` ([../design/content-archetypes.md](../design/content-archetypes.md)) and `EnemyAudioMapping`, `BossAudioMapping`, the weapon audio mapping ([../design/audio.md](../design/audio.md)) — **do not change**. The generator stays inside the existing contract; any field that cannot be expressed in those shapes is a signal for a new decision, not a local extension.
- `content-authoring.md` gains the rule "fill in, do not trim": when a `content library` ↔ presentation pair (or several layers within one area) disagree, the source of truth is the **fuller** state; missing pieces are filled in MD rather than trimmed away. This rule applies to `weapons` (the registry of `WEAPON_AUDIO_MAPPINGS` and `SampleRegistry` already include `pistol`/`shotgun`/`smg`/`sniper`/`laser` — all five archetypes go into MD, not just `pistol`) and to `enemies` (`SampleRegistry` already provides three separate pools `slimes/hit-*`, `slimes/death-*`, `slimes/voice-*` — `enemies.md` is filled in so the pools are actually used).
- The structural invariants from 011 are reused without extending the format:
  - the reference check "`id` in any balance/sound table exists as an H2 in the definitions partition" — now applies to weapons/drops/bosses too;
  - nested collections (`phases[]`, `attacks{}` on a boss; `dropTable[]` on enemies) are expressed as flat tables with the target `id` and several rows per entity — exactly like `## Drops` for enemies in 011;
  - `BossPhaseSpec.allowedAttackIds` are stored as a comma-separated list-in-cell;
  - cross-references inside an area (`phase.allowedAttackIds ⊆ ## Attacks` of the same `bossId`; `dropArchetypeId ∈ DROP_ARCHETYPES`) are checked by the area parser.
- Runtime validators (`validateEnemyRegistry`, `warnIfWeaponMayTunnel`, `validateMappings` in `createAudioMappings`, drop-table checks) keep working on top of the generated data — the generator does not duplicate or replace them.

## Out of scope

- Sessions and encounter composition (waves, breaks, the `playableModes` preset catalog, `buildSession.ts` with spawn-plan and encounter-transition descriptions) — a separate story; needs its own design decision about how waves and encounter transitions are described.
- Players (`SANDBOX_PLAYER`/`TRAINING_PLAYER`) and Arenas (`SANDBOX_ARENA`) — coupled to session composition and travel together with it.
- Presentation areas without a `content library` counterpart (`SampleRegistry`, `EVENT_AUDIO_MAPPINGS`, `WEAPON_AUDIO_MAPPINGS`-style fields not tied to archetypes) — a separate story; needs a focused `content-authoring.md` extension about MD sources for the pure presentation layer.
- Extending `DropEffect` with new `kind`s (e.g. `weapon-swap`, `speed-up`) and extending the registries beyond the listed entities (new bosses, new drop types). This story moves the existing literals into MD; new types belong to whoever introduces them as a gameplay feature.
- Per-drop and per-boss-hit/death audio channels — there is no sample seed in `SampleRegistry` and no mapping in `AudioMappings.ts`; they are introduced when files appear.
- Hot reload of MD without restarting `npm run dev`, a UI editor on top of MD, formats other than Markdown — the same out-of-scope as in 011.

## Acceptance

- The repository contains three new MD files: `content/weapons.md`, `content/drops.md`, `content/bosses.md`. Each is the single source of truth for its area, structured per [../design/content-authoring.md](../design/content-authoring.md):
  - a definitions partition (`# Weapons` / `# Drops` / `# Bosses`), with one H2 per entity and a `field | value` table for the named part (`displayName`, `color`);
  - a `# Balance` partition with narrow (≤5-column) comparison tables grouped by parameter type: for weapons — `## Cooldown`, `## Projectile`, `## Damage`, `## Sound`; for drops — `## Body` (radius/ttlMs), `## Effect` (kind/amount); for bosses — `## Body`, `## Movement`, `## Contact damage`, `## Knockback`, `## Phases` (multiple rows per `bossId`), `## Attacks` (multiple rows per `bossId`), `## Sounds`.
- `npm run content:build` regenerates every target file:
  - `src/shared/content/{weapons,drops,bosses}.generated.ts` — literals matching the existing types from the matching handwritten modules;
  - `src/main/audio/{weaponAudio,bossAudio}.generated.ts` — `WEAPON_AUDIO_MAPPINGS` and `BOSS_AUDIO_MAPPINGS` matching the existing types from `AudioMappings.ts`.
  - The consumers (`weapons.ts`, `drops.ts`, `bosses.ts`, `AudioMappings.ts`) import the generated literals. Registries, types, and validators stay in the handwritten files.
- After convergence `WEAPON_ARCHETYPES` contains **five** weapons: `pistol`, `shotgun`, `smg`, `sniper`, `laser`. Each has a distinct gameplay role expressed via `cooldownMs`/`projectileSpeed`/`projectileRadius`/`projectileTtlMs`/`damage` (not just a pistol variation): `pistol` — baseline; `shotgun` — high one-shot damage, long cooldown, low speed and short ttl; `smg` — short cooldown, low damage; `sniper` — the longest cooldown, the highest damage and projectile speed; `laser` — short cooldown, very high projectile speed, low damage, thin radius. Concrete numbers are picked by the game designer when authoring the MD.
- After generation `WEAPON_AUDIO_MAPPINGS` contains exactly the same five entries as before (a `fire` sampleId per archetype); the invariant "`id` from the audio table ⊆ `id` from the definitions partition" holds; `validateMappings(SampleRegistry)` passes without warnings about unknown sampleIds.
- After generation `DROP_ARCHETYPES` contains `heal-orb` byte-for-byte identical to the current literal; `BOSS_ARCHETYPES` contains `slime-king` (with both phases and three attacks) byte-for-byte identical to the current literal; `BOSS_AUDIO_MAPPINGS` is unchanged.
- `content/enemies.md` is filled in:
  - in `## Sounds` the `death` column for `slime-fast`/`slime-tank` uses the pool `slimes/death-1, slimes/death-2, slimes/death-3, slimes/death-4` (not `slimes/hit-*`);
  - in `## Voice` the `sampleIds` column for `slime-fast`/`slime-tank` uses the pool `slimes/voice-1, slimes/voice-2, slimes/voice-3, slimes/voice-4`.
  - After generation the `SLIME_VARIANTS` constant in `src/main/audio/enemyAudio.generated.ts` disappears: `hit`, `death`, and `voice` of every slime reference their own pools; `voice` plays at `defaultGain: 0.3` from `SampleRegistry` (as required by [../design/audio.md](../design/audio.md)).
- Game behaviour for weapons/drops/bosses does not change relative to today: only `pistol`/`heal-orb`/`slime-king` are actually used in the active presets, and their values stay the same. Slime audio behaviour does change: death and voice play their own files/volumes — that is the intended alignment with `audio.md`.
- For any error in any MD the generator fails with a clear message (file, section, line, field, what was expected) and **writes nothing** to the output files — the atomicity from 011 covers all four areas at once. Cross-reference errors (`phase.allowedAttackIds` with a non-existent `attackKey`, `dropArchetypeId` without a row in the `## Drops` partition, an `id` in balance/sound without an H2 in defs) are caught by the corresponding area parser.
- CI catches drift across all areas: `content:check` already runs first in `build` (story 011), and the new target files are picked up automatically.
- `npm test` stays green.
- Demo (weapons): the game designer opens `content/weapons.md`, changes `pistol.cooldownMs` from `250` to `120`, runs `npm run content:build` and `npm run dev`, sees noticeably faster firing in `Training` and `Escape`.
- Demo (drops): change `heal-orb.effect.amount` from `1` to `3`, rebuild, pick up a drop, see HP restored by 3.
- Demo (bosses): change `slime-king.maxHp` from `40` to `20`, rebuild, finish the `Escape` final in noticeably fewer hits.
- Demo (enemy sound): after filling in `## Voice` with `slimes/voice-*`, the game designer launches `Training` and on the slimes' ambient voice hears a clearly quieter sound than on a hit on the same slime (the actual alignment with `audio.md`: voice samples play at `defaultGain: 0.3`, hit at `1.0`).
