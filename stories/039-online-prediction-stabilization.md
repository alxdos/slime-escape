# Online Prediction Stabilization

- Status: in-progress
- Created: 2026-05-01
- Updated: 2026-05-01 (architect prep T1 landed across two review rounds. Stabilization scope decomposed across **renderer composition (PR-A)** and **predictor consistency (PR-B)**. Contract clarifications/changes recorded in [online-client-prediction.md](../design/online-client-prediction.md): (1) **replay** catches up the predictor's `simTime` from `snapshot.simTimeMs` to `max(snapshot.simTimeMs, preReconcileSimTimeMs)` (the predictor's own pre-rollback simTime), reproducing the wall-clock-progressing motion the predictor was already showing; `BufferedInput` gains `appliedAtSimTime`; (2) `lastWallMs` baseline preserved across reconcile (no lost-frame after snapshot); (3) **cooldown preservation** `max(authoritative.cooldownReadyAtSimMs, predictor.nextFireSimMs)` per weapon slot, with full replacement on `formArchetypeId` change; the merge logic lives **inside the predictor module**, the shared `CombatSystem.setPlayerWeaponHud` keeps its full-replace semantics — story 039 changes no engine behaviour; (4) **self-blend retired** for routine motion — render predicted directly, snap-on-event only; the `0.05 wu` epsilon was below per-tick motion and triggered every snapshot; any future blend layer must source `nowMs` from wall-clock; (5) **renderer interpolation formula** for non-self entities is wall-clock-driven: `latestSimTime = curr.simTimeMs + (nowWall - curr.receivedAtWall)`, `renderSimTime = latestSimTime - SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS`, lerp by α between `prev` and `curr`. Without the `nowWall` term α stays constant between snapshots and the rendered position stair-steps at 30 Hz. Entities only in `prev` are dropped immediately (parity with local renderer, no prev-only ghosts); (6) **own-projectile fallback as sequence-group rule** — bucket own projectiles by `spawnInputSequence`; for each authoritative group, hide all authoritative entries when predicted has at least one matching, otherwise show all authoritative entries. This avoids the 1:1-match deadlock for shotgun pellets and held-fire streams that share one sequence; (7) **`spawnInputSequence` is per-fire-start-command, not per-shot** — known limitation. Held fire emits multiple shots over time, all sharing one sequence; matching/fallback is intentionally coarse. A future fireInstanceId could subdivide if needed; (8) `syncOwnProjectiles` ack-window — wait one full `SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS` after sequence ack before removing a predicted projectile with no authoritative match; (9) **predictor local TTL/despawn vs fallback rule** acknowledged: predictor reuses shared `CombatSystem.tickProjectiles` which despawns by TTL/arena bounds; the renderer's own-projectile fallback is what protects against phantom-removal. (10) **Two render paths kept, contract enforced via parallel tests**: `PublicArenaRenderer` (PvP) and shared `Renderer` (online co-op) both implement the composition contract; collapse-into-one is deferred. [main-ui-shell.md](../design/main-ui-shell.md) extended with the co-op online renderer wiring rule. Tasks: T2 PvP renderer interpolation + self-direct + sequence-group own-projectile fallback, T3 co-op renderer wiring with parallel tests, T4 predictor consistency code (`preReconcileSimTimeMs` replay target, predictor-local cooldown merge), T5 verification under throttled network.)

## Product intent

Story 038 shipped the online client-side prediction infrastructure (predictor in worker, sequence-tracked inputs, authoritative reconciliation, renderer composition). Live PvP testing showed the cybersport-grade UX target ("movement of own player and own bullets feel like local play") is **not** met: the local slime moves jerkily, own projectiles flicker or vanish, other players stair-step at the snapshot rate, and online co-op (story 037) has no prediction at all because the campaign-shape online renderer never reads the predicted snapshot.

Three independent reviews agreed on the failure surface. The simulation core, sequence-tracked input contract, predictor-in-worker mode, and authoritative server policy from 034–038 are **correct**; the gaps are concentrated in the renderer composition layer and in a handful of predictor reconciliation rules that the original 038 contract under-specified or specified wrong.

This story is **stabilization, not redesign**. Closes the gaps so the cybersport-grade UX target from 038 is actually delivered. No host-side changes, no wire-shape changes, no engine changes.

## Player-facing

- Sees: in online PvP (Public Arena), other players' slimes and their bullets render as smoothly as offline single-player — no visible 30 Hz stair-step on remote entities.
- Sees: in online PvP, the local player's own slime moves smoothly under continuous input (e.g., holding W) — no per-snapshot "skid" or visible blend overshoot during normal direction changes.
- Sees: in online PvP, the local player's own bullets are visible from the moment of fire to despawn — no intermediate vanishing frames after firing.
- Sees: in online co-op vs slimes, the local player's slime has the same instant-input feel as in offline play — no full server-round-trip lag.
- Sees: form changes (level up to slime/boss in PvP, ghost / revive in co-op) still snap cleanly, no blend across the teleport.
- Sees: shotgun and other multi-projectile weapons show all pellets, not collapsed onto a single visible bullet.

## Technical

Stabilization fixes recorded in [online-client-prediction.md](../design/online-client-prediction.md) and [main-ui-shell.md](../design/main-ui-shell.md). Two clusters:

**PR-A — Presentation/reconciliation layer**:

- The PvP online renderer (`src/main/online/PublicArenaRenderer.ts`) accepts a `SnapshotPair` (prev + curr) and `getSnapshotReceivedAtMs` accessor. Per frame, it interpolates every non-self entity using **wall-clock-driven** `latestSimTime`: `latestSimTime = curr.simTimeMs + (nowWall - curr.receivedAtWall)`, `renderSimTime = latestSimTime - SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS`, `alpha = clamp01((renderSimTime - prev.simTimeMs) / (curr.simTimeMs - prev.simTimeMs))`. Without the `nowWall` term the formula yields a constant α between snapshots and reproduces the same 30 Hz stair-step the rule is closing. Entities present only in `curr` render at `curr` directly; entities only in `prev` are dropped immediately (parity with local renderer, no prev-only ghosts).
- Self position renders **directly** from the predicted snapshot every frame — no blend layer for routine motion, no `0.05 wu` epsilon test. The replay catch-up in PR-B is the only smoothing mechanism for self.
- **Sequence-group own-projectile fallback**: bucket own projectiles in both predicted and authoritative snapshots by `spawnInputSequence`. For each authoritative group `S`: if predicted has at least one projectile with `spawnInputSequence === S`, the predicted projectiles win and all authoritative `S`-group entries are hidden; if predicted has none for `S`, all authoritative `S`-group entries render. Authoritative own projectiles with `spawnInputSequence === null` always render. The group rule is the right shape for the per-fire-start `spawnInputSequence` semantics — multi-projectile patterns (shotgun) and held-fire streams both share one sequence across N shots, and 1:1 matching would either deadlock or double-render.
- The shared `Renderer` (`src/main/render/Renderer.ts`) gains an optional `getPredictedSnapshot` accessor. `UiShell.ensureOnlineCampaignRenderer` wires it through for online co-op; local-session renderer creation passes `null` (or omits). Co-op self-prediction therefore reaches the renderer instead of running uselessly in the worker. Both `PublicArenaRenderer` and the shared `Renderer` implement the same composition contract; story 039 keeps them as two physical files and enforces contract symmetry through **parallel regression tests in both files**.

**PR-B — Predictor consistency**:

- `BufferedInput` gains `appliedAtSimTime: number` recorded at `submitInput` time.
- `receiveAuthoritativeSnapshot` captures `preReconcileSimTimeMs` **before** rolling state back. `replayBufferedInputs` rewrites to catch up the predictor's `simTime` from `snapshot.simTimeMs` to `max(snapshot.simTimeMs, preReconcileSimTimeMs)` in `SIM_STEP_MS` steps, applying buffered commands at their `appliedAtSimTime`. This reproduces the wall-clock-progressing motion the predictor was already showing before reconcile clobbered it. The earlier candidate target `snapshot.simTimeMs + (nowWall - snapshotReceivedAtWall)` collapses to nearly zero when the worker forwards the snapshot immediately and is rejected.
- `receiveAuthoritativeSnapshot` no longer nulls the predictor's `lastWallMs`; the next pump call advances normally instead of no-op'ing for one frame.
- **Cooldown preservation lives inside the predictor module, not in shared `CombatSystem`**. The shared engine's `setPlayerWeaponHud` keeps its current full-replace semantics — story 039 changes no shared engine behaviour. The predictor reads its current weapon state before the engine call, computes per-slot `max(authoritative.cooldownReadyAtSimMs, predictor.nextFireSimMs)`, and synthesises a merged `WeaponHudSnapshot` to feed in. On `formArchetypeId` change the entire cooldown set is replaced from authoritative with no merge (slot identities are not comparable across forms). Modifiers are always replaced from authoritative.
- `syncOwnProjectiles` waits one full `SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS` after a sequence is acknowledged before removing a predicted projectile with no authoritative match. This bridges the race window where the server fires the projectile after the originating snapshot's `simTime` was captured.

`spawnInputSequence` is intentionally **per-fire-start-command**, not per-shot — the same sequence stamps all pellets of a shotgun burst and all autofire shots from a held LMB. Story 039 accepts this coarse semantics and operates on sequence groups (matching, fallback, removal). A future story could introduce a `Projectile.fireInstanceId` distinct from `spawnInputSequence` if per-shot identity is needed for client-side hit prediction or per-bullet event correlation.

Server-side code (`server/src/online-host/**`) is not touched. Wire shape unchanged. **No engine changes**: shared `CombatSystem.setPlayerWeaponHud` is unchanged; shared `MovementSystem` / `CombatSystem.tick` are reused as-is; only the predictor module (`OnlinePredictionCore`), the worker host (`workerController`), and the two renderer files change.

## Out of scope

- Re-architecting the simulation core, predictor world view, sequence ownership, snapshot extensions, or input contract from 034–038 — all stand.
- Server-side changes (host policy, snapshot fanout, input intake) — none.
- Wire shape changes — none. Existing `Snapshot` / `RuntimeEvent` / `host:*` envelope unchanged.
- Snapshot delta encoding or transport optimization.
- Companion prediction (companions stay snapshot-interpolated).
- Hit-determination prediction, despawn prediction, server-side lag compensation.
- Local single-player play — unchanged. The only change in `Renderer.ts` is an additive optional `getPredictedSnapshot` parameter that local play passes as `null`.
- Per-tick "input frame" protocol in place of per-command — stabilization keeps per-command.
- Quake-style rollback for hit determination, RTT bounds clamping — both stay deferred.

## Acceptance

- In online PvP (Public Arena), other players' slimes, enemies, bosses, drops, and other players' projectiles render with smooth interpolation between snapshots; visual smoothness equals offline single-player. Verified under simulated `100 ms` and `200 ms` RTT.
- In online PvP, the local player's slime moves smoothly under continuous input (held W, held W+A, etc.) without visible per-snapshot skid or jerk.
- In online PvP, the local player's own bullets are visible from the moment of fire to despawn for both single-shot and multi-projectile (shotgun spread) weapons. Verified under throttled network.
- In online co-op vs slimes, the local player's slime has instant-input feel matching offline play. The `Renderer` reads the predicted snapshot via `getPredictedSnapshot`; predictor's output is no longer dropped on the floor. **Explicit wiring test**: a `UiShell` test asserts that `ensureOnlineCampaignRenderer` constructs the shared `Renderer` with a non-null `getPredictedSnapshot` accessor that returns the worker's predicted snapshot pair (not the authoritative one). This catches the silent regression where the predictor runs in the worker but its output is never read.
- All transition events (`playerSpawn`, `playerDowned`, `playerRevived`, `host:levelUp`, `formArchetypeId` change) still snap cleanly.
- Existing tests pass without behavioral regression. New regression tests cover:
  - **Wall-clock-driven interpolation**: with `prev.simTimeMs = 100`, `curr.simTimeMs = 133`, `curr.receivedAtWall = T`, a projectile at `prev = (0, 0)` and `curr = (10, 0)` renders at `~(0, 0)` immediately on receive (`nowWall = T`), at `~(5, 0)` at `nowWall = T + 16.5 ms`, and at `~(10, 0)` at `nowWall = T + 33 ms`. The test must drive `nowWall` forward, not the snapshot pair — a fixed-`alpha` test would pass even on the broken stair-step formulation.
  - **Replay catch-up by `preReconcileSimTimeMs`**: held W for `200 ms` (one buffered move command, ack'd) produces `~(preReconcileSimTimeMs - snapshot.simTimeMs) / SIM_STEP_MS` ticks of replayed motion (typically the predictor's wall-clock lead), not `1` and not the buffer length.
  - **Pre-tick application of unacknowledged old command**: user pressed W at predictor `simTime = 80`; snapshot arrives with `simTimeMs = 90`, `lastInputSequence` does not yet include W's sequence; the W command stays in the buffer; replay's first tick at `simTime = 90` applies the W (and predictor.simTime advances with W in effect), even though W's `appliedAtSimTime = 80 < snapshot.simTimeMs`.
  - **Cooldown preserved per slot**: predictor fires slot `0` at predictor `simTime = T`; an authoritative snapshot whose `weapons[0].cooldownReadyAtSimMs` is `< T + cooldownMs` does not regress predictor's `nextFireSimMs[0]`. Slots not fired locally track authoritative exactly.
  - **Overdrive preserved per slot**: predictor's local `overdriveUntilSimMs` advanced; an authoritative snapshot whose `weapons[s].timedEffects[temporaryOverdrive].expiresAtSimMs` is older does not regress the predictor's overdrive timer.
  - **Cooldown drops on form change**: when `formArchetypeId` changes, predictor's previous-form `nextFireSimMs[s]` is dropped — no max-merge with the new form's slot identities. Same for overdrive.
  - **Predicted projectile per-projectile grace**: a sequence-acknowledged-but-missing predicted projectile survives until `predictor.simTime - projectile.spawnedAtPredictorSimMs >= SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS`. Grace is **per-projectile**, not per-sequence.
  - **Held-fire second-cooldown shot survives**: autofire scenario — shot1 with seq=42 spawns, ack'd, despawns from authoritative; predictor's cooldown expires, fires shot2 at predictor `simTime = T` reusing seq=42; snapshot at `simTime = T + ε` carries ack'd seq=42 with **empty** authoritative own-projectile group for 42 → shot2 is **not removed** because per-projectile grace not yet elapsed (`T + ε - T < SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS`). Test must drive at least two cooldown cycles to catch the regression a per-sequence-grace rule would re-introduce.
  - **Own-projectile authoritative fallback**: with predictor's `EntityStore` empty for self, an authoritative own projectile renders.
  - **Snap-on-transition still works**: `playerSpawn`, `playerDowned`, `playerRevived`, `host:levelUp`, `formArchetypeId` change all bypass any blend layer.
- The composition-rule regression tests above land in **both** `src/main/online/PublicArenaRenderer.test.ts` and `src/main/render/Renderer.test.ts` — story 039 keeps the two render paths physically separate (PvP renderer vs shared online-aware renderer) and enforces contract symmetry through parallel tests.
- Predictor non-authoritative boundary test still passes (no `RuntimeEvent` of engine kinds emitted from predictor mode).
